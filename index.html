MOVEMENT  breathing rate while moving, experimental

While you walk, each step shakes the chest far more than a breath does, so the breath-by-breath rate above pauses. This panel estimates the rate a different way. It finds your step rhythm, cancels it, and looks for the breathing rhythm in the last 30 seconds of raw sensor data. A rate is shown only when that rhythm clearly stands out, otherwise it shows --. Needs firmware 1.6 or later. Tap an activity below while you test, so every recording says what you were doing.

SLEEP  score from movement and breathing, wellness use

For a real night: put the patch on at bedtime, connect, press Record and leave this page open with the laptop on charge. In the morning stop recording, save the waveform CSV, rate how you feel and save the night. The score estimates sleep from movement and breathing only. It does not measure sleep stages and it is not a diagnosis.

Open this file in Chrome or Edge on a laptop. Bluetooth needs the patch powered and not connected to any other app. USB needs the firmware built with SERIAL_MODE 2 and the Arduino Serial Monitor closed. Demo plays a 12 minute scripted session that triggers every biomarker: a pause at 3:20 with recovery breaths, reduced effort at 5:00, irregular rhythm at 5:35 and periodic breathing from 7:00. Use 20× to see it in under a minute. Open recording re-analyses a waveform CSV saved with Record, including a whole night. Over Bluetooth the page reconnects by itself if the link drops, and the session continues. On a phone, open this page from its https address in Chrome (Android) or Bluefy (iPhone). Simulate a night builds an 8 hour night and scores it in a few seconds. Walk demo simulates standing and walking with raw sensor data, to try the Movement panel.
