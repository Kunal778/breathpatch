BreathPatch measures breathing effort from chest movement. Rate, amplitude, timing, pauses and pattern are for research and clinician review, not diagnosis. Values come only from stretches with usable signal, and every pause and flagged period says how sure it is. Bluetooth works in Chrome on Android and laptops, and in Bluefy on iPhone.
